Document ID: DOC-GEAR-002 | Category: Gear & Equipment | Version 2.8

SECTION 1: THE 3-LAYER CLOTHING SYSTEM
Mountaineering in the Himalayas requires dynamic thermal regulation. The human body produces heat and sweat during ascents and cools rapidly during rest periods.

1. Layer 1: Base Layer (Moisture Management)
- Purpose: Transport sweat away from the skin to maintain dryness.
- Materials: 100% Merino wool (200-250 gsm) or technical synthetic polyesters (Capilene/Polypropylene).
- Critical Rule: Absolutely NO COTTON. Cotton absorbs up to 27 times its weight in water, loses all insulation value when wet, and causes rapid hypothermia.

2. Layer 2: Mid Layer (Thermal Insulation)
- Purpose: Trap warm dead air generated by the body.
- Components:
  * Lightweight grid fleece jacket (100-200 weight).
  * High-loft Down Jacket with minimum 700+ Fill Power (RDS certified) or high-grade synthetic insulation (PrimaLoft Gold). For winter treks (Kedarkantha, Chadar, Brahmatal), down jacket must be rated down to -10°C or -15°C.

3. Layer 3: Outer Shell (Weather Barrier)
- Purpose: Deflect high-velocity mountain gale winds, torrential rains, and snow storms.
- Specification: 3-layer waterproof-breathable membrane (e.g., Gore-Tex Pro, eVent, Pertex Shield) with minimum 15,000mm hydrostatic head waterproof rating and 15,000 g/m²/24h breathability rating. Must feature fully taped seams and helmet-compatible storm hood.

SECTION 2: FOOTWEAR & TRACTION GEAR
1. High-Altitude Trekking Boots:
- Ankle Support: High-cut design extending above the malleolus to prevent ankle inversion sprains on moraine and scree.
- Sole: Deep-lugged Vibram rubber outsoles with rigid shank for torsional stability.
- Waterproofing: Integrated waterproof bootie (Gore-Tex or OutDry).

2. Traction Cleats & Microspikes:
- High-grade stainless steel spikes (10-12 spikes per foot) with elastomer harness. Mandatory for hard-packed ice and frozen snow (e.g., Kedarkantha summit push, Chadar river crossing).

3. Waterproof Gaiters:
- Durable Cordura nylon gaiters worn between boot top and knee to prevent snow, scree, and glacial water from entering the boot collar.

SECTION 3: EYEWEAR & UV PROTECTION
- Solar radiation increases by 10-12% per 1,000 meters elevation.
- Snow reflection (albedo) reflects up to 85-90% of harmful UV-A and UV-B rays.
- Requirement: Category 3 or Category 4 glacier sunglasses with 100% UV400 protection and side shields to prevent photokeratitis (snow blindness).
- Normal fashion sunglasses (Category 1 or 2) are completely inadequate and dangerous at altitudes above 3,500m.

SECTION 4: RUCKSACK & SLEEPING SYSTEM
- Backpack Capacity: 50L to 65L internal frame pack with ergonomic padded hip belt bearing 80% of pack weight on the iliac crest.
- Rain Cover: Integrated waterproof 210D ripstop backpack cover.
- Sleeping Bag Rating: Comfort rating of -5°C for 3-season treks and -15°C to -20°C for winter Himalayan expeditions.
