SECTION 1: TREK OVERVIEW & KEY STATISTICS
- Region: Govind Pashu Vihar National Park, Uttarkashi District, Uttarakhand
- Base Camp: Sankri Village (Altitude: 1,950m / 6,400 ft)
- Peak Altitude: 3,810 meters (12,500 feet)
- Total Distance: 20 km round-trip
- Grade: Moderate (Excellent introductory winter snow summit trek)
- Best Season: December to April for winter snow; October-November for crystal clear autumnal Himalayan vistas.

SECTION 2: DAY-BY-DAY TRAIL ITINERARY
Day 1: Dehradun to Sankri (Drive: 210 km, 8-9 hours, 1,950m)
Scenic drive via Mussoorie, Kempty Falls, Nainbagh, Damta, Purola, and Mori along the Tons River. Sankri serves as the logistics trailhead.

Day 2: Sankri to Juda Ka Talab (Trek: 4 km, 4-5 hours, Altitude Gain: 1,950m to 2,770m)
Ascend through dense pine, oak, and deodar forests. Cross small wooden bridges and gentle streams. Juda Ka Talab is an enchanting alpine lake with a small island of grass in the middle, completely frozen in peak winter.

Day 3: Juda Ka Talab to Kedarkantha Base Camp (Trek: 3.5 km, 3-4 hours, Altitude Gain: 2,770m to 3,430m)
Emerge above the tree line onto expansive alpine meadows. Incredible views of Bandarpoonch, Swargarohini, and Kala Nag (Black Peak). Camp pitching and early acclimatization check (SpO2 & Lake Louise Score).

Day 4: Kedarkantha Base Camp to Summit (3,810m) and descend to Hargaon (Trek: 6 km, 6-7 hours)
Summit push starts at 03:30 AM with headlamps. Ascend through steep snowy ridgeline using microspikes and trekking poles. Arrive at the summit by 06:30 AM for a 360-degree sunrise over 13 Himalayan peaks. Descend to Hargaon campsite (2,650m) through oak forests.

Day 5: Hargaon to Sankri and Drive to Dehradun (Trek: 6 km, 3 hours; Drive: 210 km)
Gentle downhill descent back to Sankri base camp, followed by return transport to Dehradun.

SECTION 3: MANDATORY PERMITS & FOREST CHECKPOSTS
- Forest Department Permit: Issued at the Govind Wildlife Sanctuary entry gate before Sankri.
- Mandatory Identification: Government ID proof (Aadhaar / Passport) and signed medical fitness certificate.
